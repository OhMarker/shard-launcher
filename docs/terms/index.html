Terms of Sale and refunds

Last updated October 2026.

What you are buying

Shards are a digital in-game currency for Shard, a Minecraft launcher and client made by OhMarker. You use Shards in Shard Launcher to unlock cosmetics such as capes, shields and bandanas. Cosmetics are visual only: they change how your character looks to Shard players and give no gameplay advantage.

Shards have no cash value

  • Shards cannot be exchanged for money, cashed out, sold, traded or moved to another player.
  • Shards belong to the Minecraft account you were signed in with when you bought them.
  • Prices of Shard packs and cosmetics may change. Shards and cosmetics you already have are never taken away because of a price change.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own secure page. Shard never sees or stores your card details. Prices are shown in US dollars; your bank may convert them. Your Shards are added to your account right after the payment succeeds.

Refunds

  • Because Shards are delivered instantly, purchases are final except where the law of your country gives you a right to a refund.
  • If something went wrong (you were charged but got no Shards, or you were charged twice), contact us and we will fix it or refund you.
  • If a payment is refunded or disputed with your bank, the Shards from that purchase are removed from your balance.

Players under 18

If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian before buying Shards.

Fair play

Accounts that cheat, abuse refunds or use stolen payment methods may lose their Shards and cosmetics.

Contact

Questions or problems with a purchase: open a ticket in the Shard Discord server (#support-tickets).
